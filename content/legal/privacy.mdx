## What we collect

If you join the newsletter, follow an app, post on the idea board, or unlock a guide,
we collect your **name and email address**, plus minimal anti-abuse signals (a hashed
IP, timestamp, and which form you used). We never store raw IP addresses.

## Why we collect it

To send you the updates you asked for, to operate the idea board, and to prevent abuse.

## Legal basis

Consent (for emails) and legitimate interest (for security/anti-abuse).

## Who processes it

`[Supabase]` (database), `[Resend]` (email delivery), `[Vercel]` (hosting),
`[Cloudflare]` (bot protection). Each acts as a processor under its DPA.

## Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, portability, and objection. Every email has a one-click
unsubscribe, and you can request erasure at `[privacy@yourdomain.com]`.

## Retention

Unconfirmed signups are purged after 30 days; confirmed subscribers are kept until you
unsubscribe; a suppression marker is kept indefinitely so we never email you again.
