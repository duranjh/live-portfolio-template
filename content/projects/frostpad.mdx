## The idea / why it mattered

I wanted a scratchpad that opens instantly, works offline, and never ships my notes to
someone else's server in the clear. Frostpad is that: local-first, end-to-end encrypted,
and keyboard-first.

## Challenges

Local-first sync is deceptively hard — two devices editing offline will always disagree
eventually, and you have to resolve that without quietly losing anyone's work.

## Solutions

A small append-only change log per note plus a deterministic merge, so the same edits land
the same way on every device. Encryption happens before anything leaves the machine.

## What I learned

"Instant" is a feature you have to defend constantly — every sync, encrypt, and render step
is a chance to add latency you'll regret.

## Tech & takeaways

Next.js with a local SQLite store is a surprisingly capable base for a local-first app.
