## Week 1 — Validate

Talk to five people who have the problem before you write any code.

## Week 2 — Build the spine

The smallest thing that delivers the core value. Nothing else.

## Week 3 — Make it real

Polish the one flow that matters. Cut everything else.

## Week 4 — Launch

Ship it publicly, collect emails, and start the next one.
